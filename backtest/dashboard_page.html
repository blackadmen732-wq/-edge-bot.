How the test worked

  • Kalshi's hourly price history for every settled game since May 2025, matched to the real result. Kalshi's settlement agreed with the final score in every matched game.
  • Soccer fair prices: Pinnacle where available, the Betfair exchange otherwise, taken on the Friday or Tuesday afternoon before each game. The bot bet 2 hours later, at Kalshi's asking price at that moment.
  • The bot's own code made every decision: the fee, the 3% bar, the sizing and the waiting orders. Waiting orders only filled if Kalshi's price later dropped below them.
  • NFL used the average closing price of about 20 US sportsbooks, and college football the closing price of 3, with the bot betting 1 hour before kickoff. That peeks at an hour of news, so those results flatter the bot.
  • A separate agent audited the test. It confirmed the price-gap numbers independently and found a bad odds row, missing size limits and a pricing flaw in the live bot. All three are fixed here, and the live bot now uses the fixed pricing.
  • Not tested: price moves shorter than an hour. If an edge exists, it's in the minutes after the sharp books move. Testing that needs minute-by-minute sportsbook odds, a paid data plan.
